About BizNest

Empowering Local Businesses to Go Digital

Thousands of local shops, tutors, cafés, clinics, and independent service providers rely purely on word-of-mouth because building and maintaining a traditional website is expensive and technical.

BizNest solves this by giving every small business an instant digital storefront, product catalog, WhatsApp enquiry button, and local directory presence—with zero coding required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BizNest free to use?

Yes. Creating an account, setting up your business profile, listing products/services, and receiving customer enquiries are completely free.

How do customers contact my business?

Customers can either message you directly on WhatsApp with one tap from your storefront or submit an enquiry form that goes straight to your Owner Dashboard inbox.

Where is my data stored?

In this standalone edition, all accounts, business profiles, catalogs, and customer enquiries are saved directly in your browser's local storage so it works offline without external servers.
